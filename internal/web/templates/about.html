About {{.Instance.Name}}

{{.Instance.Name}} is a self-hostable video game diary: track what you play, rate and review it, keep a journal of sessions, build lists, and follow friends. It speaks ActivityPub, so your profile and public reviews can be followed from Mastodon and other fediverse servers.

Your data

Everything is stored in a single SQLite database on the instance. You can export a full JSON copy of your logs, lists and library at any time from Settings → Import & export. There are no analytics, ads or third-party trackers.

Privacy

Profiles, logs, reviews and lists can be public, unlisted, followers-only or private. Federation is opt-in per account, and separately for statuses, reviews and lists. Blocking works in both directions.

Federation

The server publishes an actor at /users/<name>, an outbox, followers collection and a game-aware ActivityPub extension. See NodeInfo for software details.
